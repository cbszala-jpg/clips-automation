Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Clips Automation ("the app") is a private tool operated by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel Fans vs Facts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the owner's own Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload short videos to the owner's channel and to read the view counts of those videos. It uses the Google Sheets API to write those clips and their view counts into a spreadsheet the owner created.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone else. It does not access other people's YouTube accounts, Google Drive files or spreadsheets.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to upload the owner's videos and track their views, and is never transferred to third parties.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
